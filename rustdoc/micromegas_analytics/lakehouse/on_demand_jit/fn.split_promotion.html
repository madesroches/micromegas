Expand description

Picks what to merge within one covered, closed bucket: the whole of range when rows merge within the byte cap and a rebuild of range would read at most cap source bytes. Otherwise, when the rows would merge but a cap is exceeded, each child bucket of the next finer rung gets the same treatment over the rows lying inside it. A child that a row straddles is skipped, since the row cannot be split. With fewer than two rows there is nothing to merge.
