Builds the warn! line for a query-deny-list denial (Design §5/§6): the rule id, its reason, the query’s normalized fingerprint, the caller’s identity/client attribution, and the query_id. pub so a test can assert its content directly, the same way build_log_line’s content is asserted, rather than by capturing log output.
