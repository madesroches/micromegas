fair per-identity admission scheduler for queries Fair per-identity admission scheduler for FlightSQL queries.

The scheduler never rejects: every identity gets its own FIFO queue, a per-process budget caps the cost in flight, and each time capacity frees up the next query comes from the identity with the least cost in flight. It is generic over cost and identity and knows nothing about DataFusion.
