Try it

  • Click a bar to select it. Click it again, or click empty plot area, to clear.
  • Switch to Line: click within ~8px of a point. The selection survives the toggle.
  • In service_health (2 queries), each bar in a group is selectable on its own. The published row comes from that bar's query.
Pro: the selection stands out most, and the effect is familiar from BI tools. Con: the chart has to rebuild its per-bar fill arrays on every click (uPlot bakes them in), so a click costs a chart rebuild instead of a redraw. It also overrides SQL-driven per-row colors, which then can't be read while a selection is active.
